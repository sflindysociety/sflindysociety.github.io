## Code of Conduct



### FAQ
#### What should I wear?
- Wear comfortable clothing and close-toed shoes
		    
#### What if I’m clumsy?
- So are all new learners and dancers! Dancing is difficult, and we will be there to support you throughout the process of learning :)
		    
####  Do you need a partner?
- No partner needed! During the class, we will pair you with folks & you will rotate throughout 
		    
#### Do I need experience?
- No experience at all is necessary, as long as you know what music is :^)
		    
####  What is the cost?
- This class is free thanks to support from our bar; if you find yourself there, please buy a drink or two. We recommend the Lychee Martini! They have some delicious mocktails as well.
		    
####  What do I do with my bag while dancing?
- Keep it right by your seat, we pride ourselves on a safe environment and eyes will always be on your stuff to make sure it's good
		    
####  What time is the lesson? 
- 7-8pm on Thursdays at the fabulous DecoDance bar on Polk st. Social dancing afterwards until 10pm!
